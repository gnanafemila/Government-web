Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register on the platform? ▼
Click on the "Register" button in the navigation menu, fill in your details including name, email, password, address, and district, select your user type (Common User or Admin), and submit the form. You'll be able to login immediately after registration.
How can I search for schemes? ▼
After logging in to your dashboard, you'll find a search bar at the top. You can search for schemes by name, address, or description. The results will be filtered in real-time as you type.
What is the difference between User and Admin accounts? ▼
Common Users can browse, search, and view scheme details. Admins have additional privileges to add new schemes, upload scheme images, and delete existing schemes from the platform.
How do I change the language? ▼
Use the language dropdown selector in the top navigation bar. We support 5 languages: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali. Your language preference will be saved automatically.
How can I share a scheme with others? ▼
Each scheme card has a "Share" button. Click it to use your device's native share functionality. If sharing isn't available, the link will be copied to your clipboard automatically.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes, all your data is stored securely in your browser's local storage. We do not transmit your personal information to any external servers. Your password is stored securely and never displayed in plain text.
